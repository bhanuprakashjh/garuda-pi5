Floor calibration

Fix the camera in place. Mark a rectangle on the floor. Enter its measured dimensions, then click: near-left → near-right → far-right → far-left. Map origin is the near-edge centre, not the camera. Bounding-box feet are approximate; occluded feet can be inaccurate.
